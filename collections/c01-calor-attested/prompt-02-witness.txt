Extreme close still life: a GPU die laid as a relic on black calfskin, heat discoloration radiating from one corner like a bruise in silicon. Beside it, a cooling-metal wax seal impressed with a state-hash, and a thin strip of thermal paper whose only legible line is that hash. Walnut, oxidized silver tweezers, one drop of oil. Luxury, not lab. The picture must feel impossible to regenerate without that exact thermal second.
