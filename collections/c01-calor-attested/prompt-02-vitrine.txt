A dark gallery vitrine, one artifact, museum pin-light, no crowd and no wall text. The piece is a folded sheet of mirror-steel whose creases follow a temperature map: ridges where the die ran hot, flats where it idled. Etched into the glass facing the viewer, a state-hash and "CALOR / 38" in small engraved capitals, so the covenant is in the room, not in the metadata. The reflection of the hash falls across the metal. A registrar's photograph, not a render.
