A single desk-scale ingot of blackened platinum and smoked glass, photographed as a Sotheby's lot against a black void. One face is warped by a frozen thermal gradient: the hot edge blistered to a dull copper bloom, the cold edge still mirror-polished. Along the lower bevel a state-hash is struck as tiny irregular assay hallmarks pressed into metal, not set in type. "14/38" is a foundry stamp, not a print. Hard side light. The object is the only evidence the temperature existed.
